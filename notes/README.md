# Personal Notes

Free-form notes, summaries, and experiments as you work through the learning
path. Write in your own words — that's where the understanding sticks.

## Suggested uses

- Summarize each topic after you read it.
- Paste prompts that worked (and ones that didn't) with notes on why.
- Track questions you want to research later.
- Keep a running glossary of terms in your own words.
- Record small experiments and their results.

## Files

Add new Markdown files here as your notes grow, e.g.:

- `glossary.md` — your running glossary
- `prompts-that-worked.md` — saved prompts with context
- `experiments.md` — things you tried and what happened
