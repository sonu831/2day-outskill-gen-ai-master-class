# AI Tools Demonstrated (Mastermind)

The mastermind walked through practical AI tools across common workflows —
voice input, meetings, learning, research, content, and networking. The point
isn't the specific products (they change) but the **categories** and when to
reach for each.

> Tool features and pricing shift fast. Treat the list below as a map of
> categories; verify current capabilities before committing to one.

## Quick reference

| Tool | Category | What it does |
|------|----------|--------------|
| **WhisperFlow** | Voice input | Voice-to-text for faster prompting |
| **Fireflies.ai** | Meetings | AI meeting assistant — transcripts + action items |
| **NotebookLM** | Learning | Summarize sources (incl. YouTube), quizzes |
| **Claude Research** | Research | Deep multi-source research (e.g., company profiles) |
| **ChatGPT Voice Mode** | Coaching | Real-time voice practice with feedback |
| **fort.ai** | Marketing | AI-generated product ads & photo shoots |
| **Supergrow** | Content | LinkedIn post generation |
| **Happenstance** | Networking | Find referral connections in your network |

## By workflow

### Voice input — say it instead of typing
- **WhisperFlow** turns speech into text so you can dictate prompts at conversational
  speed. Great when a prompt is long or you think faster than you type. Pair with
  any chat model — voice is just the input layer.

### Meetings — capture and action
- **Fireflies.ai** joins/records meetings, transcribes them, and extracts action
  items. Use it so nothing is lost and follow-ups are concrete, not "we'll figure
  it out." Feed the transcript into a [skill](../claude/06-skills-and-sops.md) to
  turn notes into a doc or email.

### Learning — turn content into understanding
- **NotebookLM** ingests sources (articles, PDFs, **YouTube videos**) and
  summarizes them, answers questions, and generates quizzes. Ideal for studying
  the mastermind material itself: drop the session notes in and quiz yourself.

### Research — go deep on a target
- **Claude Research** runs multi-step research across sources to build a deep
  profile — e.g., a company (Razorpay): funding, products, customers, strategy.
  Reach for it when a single search isn't enough and you need synthesized,
  cited output.

### Coaching & practice — real-time feedback
- **ChatGPT Voice Mode** lets you rehearse out loud — interview prep, sales
  pitches, hard conversations — and gives live feedback. The value is the
  *loop*: practice → feedback → refine.

### Marketing — generate the assets
- **fort.ai** produces product ads and photo shoots without a shoot — product
  images, ad creative, lifestyle shots. Good for e-commerce and DTC where you
  need many variants fast.

### Content — publish consistently
- **Supergrow** generates LinkedIn posts in your voice so you can keep a cadence
  without writing from scratch each time. Pair with context engineering
  ([5 layers](../generative-ai/05-prompt-engineering.md#the-5-layers-of-context))
  so the voice stays yours.

### Networking — warm paths
- **Happenstance** maps your network to surface referral connections — who knows
  whom, and who can introduce you to a target. It turns cold outreach into warm
  intros.

## Picking a tool

- Start from the **workflow bottleneck** (typing speed? lost meeting notes? not
  studying effectively?), not the tool.
- Prefer tools that **export clean text/data** so you can pipe their output into
  a model or a skill.
- Watch **data handling** — meeting transcripts and company research can be
  sensitive; check where data goes (see
  [Limitations & Risks](../generative-ai/06-limitations-and-risks.md)).

## Recap

- Categories over products: voice input, meetings, learning, research,
  coaching, marketing, content, networking.
- Choose by bottleneck; favor tools with clean, exportable output.
- Mind data sensitivity for meetings and research.
